## CRITICAL GAPS (vs Britam, Jubilee, CIC)

### 🔴 URGENT - Blocking Competitive Launch

1. **No Agent Self-Service Portal** (Jubilee J-Force standard)
   - Agents wait for backend approval vs instant quotes/policies
   - **Impact**: 10x slower than competitors
   - **Effort**: 3-4 weeks

2. **No WhatsApp Integration** (CIC standard - 98% Kenya reach)
   - Missing primary communication channel
   - **Impact**: Lost customer engagement opportunity
   - **Effort**: 1 week

3. **No Installment Payments** (Britam Motiflex - 30% down, 70% over 11 months)
   - Full premium upfront = barrier to 30-40% of customers
   - **Impact**: Missing major market segment
   - **Effort**: 2 weeks

4. **Vehicle Make/Model Static Arrays** (needs API)
   - Hardcoded dropdowns, can't update without app release
   - **Impact**: Maintenance nightmare
   - **Effort**: 3 days

5. **Claims Approval Workflow Incomplete**
   - Can submit claims but no approval/payment process
   - **Impact**: Half-baked feature vs competitors
   - **Effort**: 1-2 weeks
